# S03_ASSETS.md — Everything Scene 3 (Day 1, Kaag Tihar) needs

Scene 3 continues straight from s02's last frame (no cut): the doorway view of the
**kausi (roof terrace) at sunrise**, brick rooftops, black water tanks and a pagoda beyond.
The crow we only heard in s02 **lands on the parapet at a leaf plate of rice and sel roti**,
pecks, and the camera **tilts down into dark alley shadow** (hidden cut into s04).
