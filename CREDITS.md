## Note on city_night (added 2026-11-19)

`city_night` mixes a CC BY-SA 4.0 recording (the Bengaluru street) in with public-domain and
CC0 sources, so the finished bed is itself **CC BY-SA 4.0** and the pages that play it must say
so, exactly as the s02/s03 frames already do for the Kathmandu valley photo. If that is not
wanted, rebuild without the Bengaluru layer: drop the `street_sa.mp3` block in
`scripts/build_audio.py` and the bed becomes public domain + CC0 only. The city is carried by
the two `cori` recordings; the Bengaluru layer only adds the geography.
