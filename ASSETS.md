# ASSETS.md — Free assets, poly targets, licences

Free only: CC0, CC BY, or explicitly free licences. No pirated assets.
When an asset is added, record it in `docs/CREDITS.md` the same day.
The full researched list with links is in the "Assets" tab of the Claude Doc.

## Poly targets

| Target | Triangles | Textures | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High | 50k+ or subdivision-ready | 4K | Close-ups that fill the screen |
| Mid | 10k–50k | 2K + normal map | Whole object in frame |
| Low | <10k | 1K | Background, or instanced hundreds of times |

- High poly can be decimated down, but check it has UVs and textures first
  (3D-print and scan models often have neither).
- **Never decimate a rigged model.** For animals, use a rigged mid-poly model +
  Subdivision, or bind a static high-poly mesh to it with **Surface Deform**.

and tile materials might help; Poly Haven covers that already.
